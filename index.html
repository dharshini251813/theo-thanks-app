For My Role Model 💗

Dear Theo Anna, ❤️

Honestly, I don't even know how to begin this letter. There is so much I want to say, so much I feel thankful for, and so many things that words can never completely explain.

You are my favourite senior, but to me, you are more than just a senior. You are someone I genuinely admire, someone I look up to, and someone I see as a role model. The respect I have for you comes from the place you hold in my life and the person I see you as.

Sometimes, God gives us beautiful gifts in unexpected ways. I feel genuinely blessed that our paths crossed and that I got the chance to know someone like you. You are one of those people I never want to take for granted.

Thank you for being a part of my life, Anna. Thank you for every little thing, every moment, every bit of care, and everything you have done, whether big or small. Even if you don't realise how much it means to me, I am truly grateful.

I may not always express my feelings properly. I may not find the right words when I want to thank you. But that doesn't mean my gratitude is small. Sometimes, the people we appreciate the most are the very people we struggle to describe in words.

You inspire me in a way that makes me want to learn, grow, and become a better version of myself. Having someone I can respect and look up to is something I value deeply. I hope you always know that your presence and example matter.

And beyond the respect I have for you as my senior and role model, there is also the affection I have for you as my brother. We may not share the same blood, but I believe that a genuine bond can be meaningful in its own beautiful way.

I don't know what the future holds or where life will take us. We may get busy, our responsibilities may change, and our journeys may take different directions. But I hope I will always remember this bond with gratitude and respect.

If I could make you understand just one thing, it would be this: I am genuinely thankful that you came into my life. Not just because you are someone I admire, but because you are someone I appreciate as a person and as my brother.

Thank you for everything, Theo Anna. Thank you for being my role model. Thank you for being one of the unexpected blessings in my life. ❤️

With endless gratitude and respect,
Your little sister,
Dharshini 💗
